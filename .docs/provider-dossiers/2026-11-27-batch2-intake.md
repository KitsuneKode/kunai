## Implementation Decision

Advance FileSuN and AnimeParadise as narrow fallbacks. Neither meets the full
multi-server, multi-audio, 4K target on the sampled titles. Advertise only the
stream, quality and subtitle rows actually exposed. No torrent, debrid, browser
session, CAPTCHA bypass, or Kunai media relay is needed. Both provide direct
HTTP media plus separate subtitle URLs, compatible with the
[Stremio stream object](https://github.com/Stremio/stremio-addon-sdk/blob/master/docs/api/responses/stream.md)
model; their upstream discovery protocols are not themselves Stremio addons.
