Add experimental Google Cast playback routing, secured session media delivery, transport controls, and synchronized local-video/remote-audio output.

### Highlights

- Choose local playback, full Google Cast A/V, or ffmpeg-extracted remote audio with local video.
- Pause, resume, seek by 10 seconds, or jump with the interactive seek bar.
- Proxy header-protected streams through a tokenized gateway scoped to one playback session.
