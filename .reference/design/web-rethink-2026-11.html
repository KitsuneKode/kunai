Ranked proposals

Put the product on the first screen

Order the hero as headline, one line, install command, then the terminal stage rising above the fold (as mocked above). Merge the four scene cards into the stage's lane tabs instead of a deck above the headline. Drop the second button and move the spec strip to the open-source section.

home-page-shell.tsx, hero-scene-ribbon.tsx, home-terminal-island.tsx · Effort M · References: Agno and Dia on Vibrant (real product UI rising into the first screen); Imageory (centred headline over one row of media at the fold).

Three body sizes, one scale

Replace the 21 sizes with tokens: --text-body: 15px, --text-sm: 13px, mono at 13px. The h1 and the wordmark are exempt. Nothing under 13px above the fold.

app/styles/typography.css, home.css · Effort M · Risk: touches every section, so check it in a browser pass.

A five-link nav with a stronger wordmark

Docs, Releases, Analytics, Support, plus search. Move Debug under Docs, Disclaimer to the footer, and Install to the hero anchor. Set the wordmark at 20px/600 against 14px links.

app/layout.config.tsx, nav-compact.tsx · Effort S

Show the real pipeline as steps that tick

Replace "From search to mpv in three steps" with four true stages: search, providers race, stream probed, mpv. Each ticks once when it scrolls into view. This is what Kunai does that a browser can't, and the probe step is the honest differentiator.

components/home/*steps* · Effort M · References: Agent steps and Task list via DesignBookmark Motion.

A 3px sakura progress line at the bottom of the terminal stage, drawn once on view to "resume at 18:42". It makes resume and history visible without a feature card.

What not to take

  • Modal's neon wireframe cube and Aceternity-style beams and spotlights: generic AI-infra glow.
  • Imageory's pastel painted imagery: we're a terminal tool, not an image library.
  • Liquid glass and refraction cards (several DesignBookmark and Vibrant entries): they fight the flat sumi surfaces.
  • Logo clouds and testimonial walls: Kunai has no customers to list, and inventing them would be dishonest.
  • Pricing-style sponsor tiers above the fold: sponsorship stays in Support.
  • Big stat counters. "11 direct providers" belongs in a sentence, not a KPI strip.
