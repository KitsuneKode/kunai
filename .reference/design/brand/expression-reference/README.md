# Expression reference — batch 1

**These are not a shipping source.** Nothing generates from them, nothing points
at them, and no export reads this directory. They are here for one reason: the
eye and brow shapes.

## Why they came back

`093a720d` deleted them, reasoning that "leaving 5.3 MB of superseded art beside
the set that replaced it is how the next person picks the wrong one." That was
right while they were superseded.

They stopped being superseded when it turned out the thing they had — expression
— was the thing the replacement lost. Batch 1 drew three separate _directions_
(Operator, Courier, Watcher), so the ear silhouette, eye treatment and chest
blaze changed between poses and it never read as one animal. That is the failure
Kanna exists to fix, and it is not up for revisiting.

But the batch-1 `idle` and `watch` faces carry a **narrow, slanted, tapered eye**
that reads as unimpressed, and the Kanna sheet has nothing like it. Squashing a
round eye is not the same shape: it stays an ellipse, and at nav and favicon size
the difference all but vanishes.

## What to take, and what not to

Take: the eye outline and the brow angle on `A1` / `C1`. That is the whole brief.

Do not take: the construction. Not the ears, not the head ratio, not the muzzle,
not the shading. Those are Kanna's and they are settled — see
[kanna-art-brief.md](../kanna-art-brief.md).

The vocabulary they feed is
[kanna-expressions.mjs](../kanna-expressions.mjs). An expression is a path in
that file, not a new master and not a transform of an existing shape.
