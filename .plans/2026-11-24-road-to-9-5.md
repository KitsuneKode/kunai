## What the competitors that are growing do (researched 2026-11-24)

| Project      | Stars  | Age           | What drives it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ------ | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ani-cli      | 14,156 | since 2021    | One shell script in every distro's repos; does one thing; Syncplay, rofi, Steam Deck                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| streambert   | 7,210  | since 2026-02 | Electron app; screenshots fill the README; asks each user for their own free TMDB key with a tutorial; mirrored on Codeberg; AUR and .deb/.AppImage/.pacman                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| MovieBox-TUI | 2,905  | since 2026-07 | Rust/ratatui; video walkthrough first in the README; Homebrew tap and Scoop bucket in its own repo; curl/irm/Termux/cargo installs; Stremio addons; IPTV with EPG; resolution and subtitle pickers before play; mpv/VLC/IINA; batch downloader; 9 themes; "zero telemetry"; READMEs in Bengali, Hindi and Spanish; a Telegram channel; a release every 1–2 weeks (16–31k downloads each) |
| Seanime      | 4,347  | since 2023    | Self-hosted anime server with AniList, torrents and debrid; web and desktop                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Kunai already matches or beats MovieBox-TUI on recovery, resume, sync, posters,
diagnostics and engineering. It loses on everything a new user sees first:
no recent release, no package-manager install, no demo, scrapers that fail
today, and no addon or live-TV story. W1, W4 and W5 close those; the README
pass and the launch clip follow once a release with healthy providers exists.
