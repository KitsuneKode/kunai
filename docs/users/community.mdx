Kunai gets better every time someone reports a dead provider, shares a
working one, or builds a new lane. This page is the meeting point: live
health, an honest capability guide, and the paths to contribute.

## What works right now

A scheduled job resolves a known-good title through every production provider
once a day from a clean network. Full detail lives on the
[provider status](/docs/users/provider-status) page; the short version:

<ProviderStatusBoard />

Upstream health, not your network's view: a region-gated provider may work
fine for you through a user-owned relay, and Kunai's automatic fallback
already routes around failures while you watch.

## Which provider shines at what

No single lane is best at everything. Pick by what you care about tonight:

| Lane                                                 | Quality switcher                             | Dub / sub                              | Servers                               | Subtitles                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Movies & series (VidLink, Rivestream, Movy, VidRock) | Up to 4K ladders where the lane carries them | Original audio                         | Multi-server lanes on most providers  | Soft subtitles on most lanes |
| Anime sub                                            | Full ladders                                 | Japanese + English dub where available | Server choice on supporting providers | Soft VTT/ASS tracks          |
| Anime dub                                            | Full ladders                                 | English dub                            | Server choice on supporting providers | Soft subs alongside dub      |
| YouTube                                              | Your quality preference, up to source max    | Original                               | Player-client failover lanes          | Auto-attached captions       |

In the shell, `/providers` switches lanes, `o` switches sources, `k` switches
quality, and the subtitle picker lists every track the lane resolved. If a
provider is down for everyone, switch it off in `/providers` → "Turn
providers on/off" and it stays out of your way until it recovers. See
[providers](/docs/users/providers) for fallback and priority.

## Request a provider

Want a site supported? The most useful request includes evidence:

1. Play something nearby and run `/diagnostics` → export the redacted bundle.
2. Open an issue with the site URL, a sample title (one movie, one series
   episode if supported), and whether you expect subs or dub.
3. Note anything odd: login walls, click-to-play overlays, per-server
   differences.

A request with a diagnostics bundle gets researched first; a bare site name
waits until someone reproduces it. What gets researched is tracked openly:
dead ends (gated, down, or browser-only backends) are recorded too, so nobody
repeats the work.

## Build a provider

Providers are standalone modules behind a stable contract: search, episode
listing, and stream resolve with subtitles, quality, and audio evidence. A
community plugin index is on the roadmap. Authors will scaffold from a
template, run the same contract suite the built-in lanes pass (including the
rule that a provider never reports success for a stream it has not probed),
and submit with a short research dossier. Until the index opens, the path is:

1. Read [contribute](/docs/developer/contribute) for workflow and standards.
2. Prototype against the provider contract and the pinned-title smoke pattern.
3. Open a PR with the module, fixtures, and dossier. Review covers the
   contract, not your code style.

## Share Kunai

The fastest growth is a link that plays: `kunai://` share links open a title
directly in someone else's client. See [share links](/docs/users/share-links).
If Kunai just works for you, that link plus one sentence is the whole
marketing department.
