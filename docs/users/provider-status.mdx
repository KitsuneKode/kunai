A scheduled job tries a fixture title through every production provider once a day from the hosted runner's network. Access and resolve times can differ on your network.

<ProviderStatusBoard />

## What the statuses mean

- **Working** means the fixture returned streams before the default attempt deadline.
- **Limited** means the provider exhausted its sources or returned them after the default deadline (`degraded`), or the resolver observed an access refusal (`blocked`).
- **Down** means the front door reported maintenance (`down`) or failed to answer and the resolver returned no streams or refusal (`dead`).

The sweep gives each provider up to 30 seconds to resolve. The note distinguishes a resolve within that window from one that also fit the default 12-second attempt budget. Qualification measures the fixture's resolver through stream discovery and ends before the player opens the stream.

## How this affects playback

You do not need this page to have a good experience. Kunai's provider-health tracking already demotes a provider that fails, and [fallback cycling moves to the next candidate automatically](/docs/users/providers). This board exists so you can tell _why_ a provider is struggling and whether it is you or them.

If your configured default provider shows **Down** here for days, pick another in [Settings → Sources](/docs/users/customization); the board shows which ones are working right now. When nothing resolves at all, that is a different failure class: see [playback and recovery](/docs/users/playback-and-recovery) for the fallback chain and [diagnostics](/docs/users/diagnostics-and-reporting) for the redacted support bundle.
