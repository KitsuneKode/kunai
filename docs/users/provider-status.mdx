A scheduled job resolves a known-good title through every production provider once a day from a clean network and publishes the result here. This board is **upstream health**, not your network's view — a region-gated provider may work fine for you (or vice versa).

<ProviderStatusBoard />

## What the statuses mean

- **Healthy** — upstream reachable and a real resolve returned playable lanes.
- **Degraded** — upstream responds but the resolve exhausted without a clear block reason; usually a scheme rotation or partial outage.
- **Region-gated** — upstream alive but challenges the sweep network (Cloudflare WAF, captcha). Often works from other regions or through a user-owned relay (`providerRelay.baseUrl`).
- **Maintenance** — upstream itself reports maintenance (503 pages).
- **Unreachable** — DNS or connection failure at the upstream front door; the provider cannot resolve for anyone.

## How this affects playback

You do not need this page to have a good experience — Kunai's provider-health tracking already demotes a provider that fails, and [fallback cycling moves to the next candidate automatically](/docs/users/providers). This board exists so you can tell *why* a provider is struggling and whether it is you or them.

If your configured default provider shows **Maintenance** or **Unreachable** here for days, pick another in [Settings → Provider order](/docs/users/customization) — the board shows which ones are healthy right now. When nothing resolves at all, that is a different failure class — see [playback and recovery](/docs/users/playback-and-recovery) for the fallback chain and [diagnostics](/docs/users/diagnostics-and-reporting) for the redacted support bundle.
