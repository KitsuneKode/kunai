A scheduled job resolves a known-good title through every registered provider once a day from a clean network and publishes the result. This is **upstream health**, not your network's view: a region-gated provider may work fine for you (or the other way round). The same board, full width, is on the [status page](/status).

## How this affects playback

You do not need this page to have a good experience. Kunai's provider-health tracking already demotes a provider that fails, and [fallback cycling moves to the next candidate automatically](/docs/users/providers). This board exists so you can tell _why_ a provider is struggling and whether it is you or them.

If your configured default provider shows **Maintenance** or **Unreachable** for days, pick another in [Settings → Provider order](/docs/users/customization); the board shows which ones are healthy right now. When nothing resolves at all, that is a different failure class: see [playback and recovery](/docs/users/playback-and-recovery) for the fallback chain and [diagnostics](/docs/users/diagnostics-and-reporting) for the redacted support bundle.
