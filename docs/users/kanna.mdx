A **kanna** (鉋) is a Japanese hand plane. You run it over rough wood and the roughness leaves in one curl.

Kunai is the blade. Kanna is who holds it.

  Twelve tabs, three dead mirrors, a 480p stream that stalls — she runs the plane over it once and gives you the thing that plays.

## She is deliberately brief

Kanna works nights. She is not a viewing companion and she will not watch with you.

That restraint is the point, not a limitation. Kunai's whole promise is getting out of your way, and a mascot that chatters in your terminal contradicts the product it belongs to. So she is never excited — her range runs from calm, to focused, to mildly put-out. A full session from launch to quit shows at most four lines from her.

If she ever feels like she is talking too much, that is a bug. [Open an issue](https://github.com/KitsuneKode/kunai/issues).

## Where she shows up

She appears on the surfaces that would otherwise leave you looking at nothing — an empty list, a failed resolve, the setup wizard from [Getting Started](/docs/users/getting-started), and the goodbye screen. She stays quiet while something is loading or when a step already succeeded, because those already tell you what is happening.

```text
·  No results for "dune 2099"
   Try a different spelling, or switch catalog.
   🦊 empty for now.
```

```text
×  Could not resolve a stream
   3 providers tried · allanime, miruro, vidlink
   🦊 couldn't get there.
```

## Poses

Each pose maps to something real that is happening, so she never becomes decoration.

| Pose | Reads as | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | sitting, ears up, eyes soft | terminal: at rest and on the setup summary · site: home, social cards |
| `wait` | square and composed | terminal: the setup wizard · site: empty shelves, install |
| `go` | mid-motion, ears swept back | site only: searching, resolving, install pages |
| `watch` | knowing, one ear tipped | terminal: playback · site: docs hub |
| `oops` | ears flat, brows angled | terminal: a failed resolve · site: 404s |
| `nap` | curled, eyes closed | terminal: the goodbye screen · site: the roamer asleep |

All six ship, drawn from one locked character. Two more — `seek` and `peek` —
are drawn but held back for a redraw, so a resolve currently borrows `go`.

## Two tiers, and neither is required

Kanna has a picture tier and a text tier, and **the text tier is the one that matters**. The illustrated fox needs a terminal that can host a graphics protocol — Kitty, Ghostty, iTerm2, or WezTerm. Which terminals qualify on your system is covered in [Platforms](/docs/users/platforms). Everywhere else she is a `🦊` and a line of text, which is the same character doing the same job.

Half-block rendering is deliberately skipped. This art turns to noise at two pixels per cell, and a smear is worse than a glyph.

## Turning her off

Three states, and the way out is a real way out.

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| unset | the picture where your terminal can draw it, the glyph everywhere else |
| `KUNAI_PET=glyph` | keep the companion, but only ever as `🦊` |
| `KUNAI_PET=off` | retire her entirely — no picture, no glyph, no lines |

```bash
KUNAI_PET=off kunai
```

`KUNAI_PET=off` silences the text too, not just the artwork. Redirected output — a pipe, a redirect, a captured log — never gets either, so nothing decorative can end up in a file or break a parser.

`KUNAI_POSTER=0` turns off images without retiring her, since the glyph is not an image. Both variables sit alongside the rest of the environment overrides in [Customization](/docs/users/customization).

### On this site

The fox that walks around these pages is a separate switch from `KUNAI_PET`, because it is a separate thing: one is your terminal, the other is a website you are visiting.

Hover her and an `×` appears at her ear. It hides her, and the preference is stored in the browser you are using — so hiding her in Firefox leaves her walking in Chrome, and clearing site data brings her back everywhere.

<KannaRoamerToggle />

Three ways back, because a click on a small button over a moving fox is a thing people do by accident:

- The **undo** she leaves in the corner for twelve seconds after you hide her.
- The toggle above, which reads the state on whichever browser you are reading in and flips it. She reacts immediately — no reload.
- **`?kanna=on`** on any page of this site. `?kanna=off` sends her away the same way, and both stick.

None of them override the two rules below her: she never appears on a touch device, and she is not rendered under reduced motion.

## Reduced motion

Every animation on this site and in the terminal is gated on your system's reduced-motion preference. With it on, the illustrated stills hold still — she does not breathe, lean, or react.

The roaming fox is the exception: she is not frozen, she is not rendered at all. Something that follows your pointer has no still version worth showing, so `KunaiFoxRoamer` returns nothing under reduced motion and the stylesheet hides her as a second guard. Every other surface keeps its artwork; only the movement stops.
